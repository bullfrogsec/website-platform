Pick the course for what you do with AI agents

If you use AI agents

Securing your coding agents

  • 1 day
  • On-site
  • Hands-on labs

For developers who use Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot or Codex in their daily work, letting it read the repo, run commands and install tools.

Questions it answers

  • What can my coding agent reach on my machine?
  • How can a repo, an issue or a web page take it over?
  • Which MCP servers, skills and extensions can I trust?
  • How do I give it room to work without handing it my keys?

If you build AI agents

Building secure AI agents

  • 2 days
  • On-site
  • Hands-on labs

For developers and security engineers building agents into your product or internal tools, for employees or for customers.

Questions it answers

  • Where can our agent be attacked, and what could it leak?
  • Which design patterns stop injected instructions from becoming actions?
  • How do we limit its credentials and what it can send out?
  • Is it ready to face customers?

If you lead the teams

AI agent risk for leaders

  • 2 hours
  • On-site or remote

For executives, CISOs and engineering leaders who decide where agents are used, and need the picture without the technical detail.

Questions it answers

  • What changes when agents act with our people’s access?
  • Why can’t the AI providers just fix this?
  • Which agents can we let run unattended?
  • What should we be asking our teams?

How every course works

Mostly hands-on

Short explanations, then labs. Attendees see how an agent gets turned against its users, then build the controls that keep data in even when the model is fooled.

Shaped around your agents

We adapt the examples and exercises to the agents and tools your team actually uses.

Built on OWASP

The courses follow the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications, so your team shares a common language with security and vendors.

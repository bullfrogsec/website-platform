Agent security assessment

Find where your agents
could leak your data.

Agents get connected to your systems by different teams, with whatever credentials were at hand. Our assessment looks at the agents and use cases that matter most to you, shows you where the risk is, and helps you reduce it.

Get in touch

How the assessment works

  1. Inventory

    The agents in scope: what each one reaches and what it can do.

  2. Threat model

    Trust boundaries, tool permissions, and the damage if an agent is turned against you.

  3. Testing

    We test your agents against real attacks, with your permission: prompt injection, tool misuse, data leaks. You see what worked and what held.

  4. Findings

    Rated by severity, with a remediation plan in order of priority.

What you get

  • An inventory of the agents in scope
  • A threat model per agent
  • Test results
  • Findings rated by severity
  • A remediation plan, in order of priority
  • A one-page summary for leadership

Your team can run the plan on its own. If you'd like a hand, we can also help put the fixes in place.
